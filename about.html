About

A vanilla-only field guide.

The Survivor's Codex catalogs every resource, weapon, recipe, block, and trap that exists in 7 Days to Die — straight out of the box, with no mods, conversions, or third-party content.

What's in here

You'll find weapons (melee and ranged), ammunition, tools, food and drink, medical items, raw resources, building blocks, traps and turrets, vehicles, clothing and armor, and item mods. Each entry shows the tier, stack size, weight, the perk that boosts it, the recipe inputs you'll need, and the situations where the item shines. The planner lets you allocate perk points and see which catalog items your build unlocks.

What's not

No modded items. No console-only spawns. No overhauls. If something can't be acquired in a default unmodded playthrough on the latest base game build, it isn't here. If we missed something or got a detail wrong, that's on us — flag it via the feedback link below.

Why we built it

Most existing wikis are exhaustive but heavy. This codex is the opposite: a fast, modern, mobile-friendly index you can open between waves, ignore the trivia, and find what you actually need to craft, loot, or stockpile.

How accounts work

Sign in with an email and password — that's all we keep. Your account exists so the site remembers you across devices. The actual favorites and uploaded photos stay on your device in localStorage. We don't ship them to a server, we don't share them, and you can clear them by signing out and clearing site data.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?, enter your account email, and use the one-time reset link shown there (valid for one hour). You can also open that link on the reset password page.
